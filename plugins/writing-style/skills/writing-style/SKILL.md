# House writing style

## When to use

Anything a person outside the team will read.

## Rules

- Say what happened, then what it means. Not the other way round.
- One claim per sentence. A sentence carrying two claims hides the weaker one.
- No hedging where a fact is known, and no confidence where it is not.
- Numbers with their units and their basis. "40% faster" without a baseline says nothing.
- No filler openers. Start at the first thing the reader does not already know.
